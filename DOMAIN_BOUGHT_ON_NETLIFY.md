# 🌐 Domain Bought on Netlify - Your Options

## Situation

You bought `mikyhillsidelodge.com` directly through Netlify, not a separate domain registrar.

---

## ✅ Good News

**You still own the domain!** Even if bought through Netlify, you can:
- Keep using it on Netlify
- Transfer it to another registrar
- Point it to another hosting service (like Vercel)

---

## 📋 Your Options

### Option 1: Keep Domain on Netlify, Point to Vercel (EASIEST)

**How it works:**
- Domain registration stays on Netlify (you keep paying them for domain)
- But DNS points to Vercel (site hosted on Vercel)

**Steps:**
1. Deploy site to Vercel
2. In Netlify:
   - Go to Domains → mikyhillsidelodge.com
   - DNS Settings
   - Update A record to point to Vercel's IP
   - Update CNAME to point to Vercel
3. Site runs on Vercel, domain managed by Netlify

**Pros:**
- ✅ Easy (just update DNS)
- ✅ Keep domain on Netlify (familiar)
- ✅ No transfer needed

**Cons:**
- ❌ Still pay Netlify for domain (~$12-15/year)
- ❌ Two services to manage

---

### Option 2: Transfer Domain Away from Netlify (MORE COMPLEX)

**Transfer to a registrar like:**
- Namecheap (cheap, ~$10/year)
- Google Domains / Cloudflare (reliable)
- GoDaddy (popular)

**Steps:**
1. Unlock domain in Netlify
2. Get transfer authorization code
3. Initiate transfer at new registrar
4. Pay transfer fee (~$10-15)
5. Wait 5-7 days for transfer
6. Point DNS to Vercel

**Pros:**
- ✅ Full control of domain
- ✅ Often cheaper
- ✅ Can use Cloudflare DNS (free, fast)

**Cons:**
- ❌ Takes time (5-7 days)
- ❌ More complex process
- ❌ Site down during transfer if not careful

---

### Option 3: Stay on Netlify, Upgrade Sanity (SIMPLEST)

**Just upgrade Sanity, keep everything as is:**
- Pay $9/month for Sanity
- Keep site on Netlify
- Keep domain on Netlify
- Everything works immediately

**Pros:**
- ✅ Simplest - no migration
- ✅ Everything works NOW
- ✅ No DNS changes needed
- ✅ No risk

**Cons:**
- ❌ Netlify build limits still exist
- ❌ Might hit limits again in future
