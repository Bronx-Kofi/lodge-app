## 📸 Image Guidelines

### Recommended Images to Upload:

1. **Wide-angle room shot** - Show the full space (1920x1080px minimum)
2. **Bed detail** - Focus on bedding quality and comfort
3. **View from room** - Highlight the scenery (golden hour lighting best)
4. **Bathroom** - Clean, well-lit shot of facilities
5. **Unique features** - Terrace, special decor, amenities
6. **Detail shots** - Local artwork, textiles, design elements

### Image Tips:
- Use natural daylight when possible
- Keep images under 5MB for faster loading
- JPG format preferred
- Horizontal orientation works best
- Show the room clean and styled

---

## 🎬 Video Guidelines

### Video Content Ideas:

1. **Room Tour** (30-60 seconds)
   - Walk through entrance
   - Pan across main features
   - Show view from balcony/terrace
   - Highlight unique amenities

2. **View Time-lapse** (15-30 seconds)
   - Sunrise or sunset from room
   - Day-to-night transition
   - Weather changes over hills

3. **Welcome Message** (20-40 seconds)
   - Host greeting guests
   - Brief room introduction
   - Personal touch

### Video Specs:
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264 codec)
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (1080p minimum)
- **Duration**: 30-90 seconds ideal
- **File Size**: < 30MB for uploads, unlimited for YouTube/Vimeo
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (horizontal)

## 💡 Pro Tips for Adding Rooms

### Do's:
✅ Use high-quality, well-lit photos
✅ Write descriptive, engaging copy
✅ Highlight unique features prominently
✅ Include accurate WiFi credentials
✅ Add helpful guest instructions
✅ Set realistic capacity numbers
✅ Price competitively for the market

### Don'ts:
❌ Don't use stock photos (guests want to see YOUR rooms)
❌ Don't oversell—be honest about amenities
❌ Don't forget the fallback image for videos
❌ Don't skip the intro—it's the first thing guests read
❌ Don't use overly large image files (optimize first)

---

## 🚀 Quick Add Workflow

1. **Go to Studio**: http://localhost:3002/studio
2. **Click "Rooms"** in the sidebar
3. **Click "Create" button** (+ icon)
4. **Fill in basic info** (title, slug, price, capacity)
5. **Write compelling intro** and description
6. **Add amenities** one by one
7. **Upload gallery images** (3-6 recommended)
8. **Add one video** (optional but impressive)
9. **Include instructions** (3-5 sections)
10. **Add WiFi details**
11. **Click "Publish"** 🎉
12. **View on frontend**: http://localhost:3002/rooms/[your-slug]
