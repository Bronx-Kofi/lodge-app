## 🔍 Troubleshooting

### Issue: "Page not found" or "404 error"
**Solution:** 
- Check Vercel deployment is complete (see Vercel dashboard)
- Try again in 5 minutes (deployment may still be processing)
- Clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+R)

### Issue: Receipt shows but missing information
**Solution:**
- Make sure you filled nationality and passport in check-in form
- If missing, contact lodge to update your booking

### Issue: Can't download PDF
**Solution:**
- Use "Print" button → Select "Save as PDF" in print dialog
- Try different browser (Chrome, Firefox)
- Disable browser pop-up blocker

### Issue: Embassy questions the document
**Solution:**
- Embassy can verify by calling lodge directly
- Provide booking reference: CHK-801898PN6
- Lodge available 24/7 for verification
