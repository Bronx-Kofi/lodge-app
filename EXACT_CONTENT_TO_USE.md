### Site 1: Boabeng-Fiema Monkey Sanctuary

**Site Name**:
```
Boabeng-Fiema Monkey Sanctuary
```

**Description**:
```
Home to over 700 sacred monkeys living freely in a protected forest. This unique sanctuary offers the rare opportunity to walk among Mona and Black-and-White Colobus monkeys in their natural habitat. The monkeys are considered sacred by local communities and have lived in harmony with villagers for centuries.
```

**Location**:
```
Boabeng-Fiema, 25 km from Miky Hillside Lodge
```

**Distance from Lodge**:
```
25 km (30-minute drive)
```

**Entry Fee**:
```
GHS 20 for adults, GHS 10 for children
```

**Best Time to Visit**:
```
Early morning (7-9 AM) when monkeys are most active
```

**Site Name**:

```
Kintampo Waterfalls
```

```
One of Ghana's highest and most beautiful waterfalls, cascading 70 meters over multiple tiers. The surrounding lush forest and natural pools create a perfect setting for swimming, picnicking, and photography. The site holds cultural significance and offers stunning views from various lookout points.
```

**Location**:
```
Kintampo, 40 km from Miky Hillside Lodge
```

**Distance from Lodge**:

```
40 km (45-minute drive)
```

**Entry Fee**:
```
GHS 15 for adults, GHS 5 for children
```
