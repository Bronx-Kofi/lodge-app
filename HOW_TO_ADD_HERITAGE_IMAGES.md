# 📸 How to Add Images to Heritage Sites

## 🎯 Quick Guide

You can add images to your heritage sites (like Kintampo Waterfalls) directly through Sanity Studio!

---

## 🖼️ Two Types of Images

### **1. Main Image** (Required)
- This is the hero image shown at the top
- Shows on cards in the Explore page grid
- Shows on the detail page header

### **2. Gallery Images** (Optional)
- Multiple images (5-10 recommended)
- Show different angles and views
- Displayed in the gallery section on detail page

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step: Adding Images

### **Step 1: Open Sanity Studio**
Go to: **http://localhost:3333**

### **Step 2: Navigate to Heritage Sites**
1. Click: **"Heritage Site"** in the left sidebar
2. You'll see your existing sites (like Kintampo Waterfalls)

### **Step 3: Open Your Site**
1. Click: **"Kintampo Waterfalls"** (or any site)
2. You'll see all the fields for that site

---

## 📷 Adding the Main Image

### **Find the "Main Image" Field**
It's near the top of the form, after Title and Category.

### **Upload a New Image**:
1. Click: **"Select"** button in the Main Image field
2. You'll see two options:
   - **Upload** - Upload from your computer
   - **Select** - Choose from previously uploaded images

### **To Upload New**:
1. Click: **"Upload"**
2. Choose: Your image file from computer
3. Wait: For upload to complete
4. The image appears in the preview

### **Image Specifications**:
- **Size**: 1920x1080 pixels (landscape) or 1200x800
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **File size**: Under 2MB (compress if needed at Squoosh.app)
- **Subject**: Clear view of the heritage site

---

## 🎨 Adding Gallery Images (Multiple Photos)

### **Find the "Gallery Images" Field**
Scroll down to find "Gallery Images (5-10 photos)"

### **Add First Image**:
1. Click: **"+ Add item"** button
2. Click: **"Select"** on the new image item
3. Click: **"Upload"**
4. Choose: Your image file
5. Wait: For upload
6. Fill in (optional but recommended):
   - **Alternative Text**: "Kintampo Waterfalls main cascade"
   - **Caption**: "The 70-meter waterfall during rainy season"

### **Add More Images**:
1. Click: **"+ Add item"** again
2. Repeat the process for each image
3. Add 5-10 images showing:
   - Main waterfall view
   - Close-up of cascades
   - Natural pools
   - Surrounding forest
   - Visitors at viewpoints
   - Different angles
   - Path/access areas

### **Reorder Gallery Images**:
- **Drag and drop** the image blocks to reorder them
- First image will appear first in gallery

### **Delete Gallery Images**:
- Click the **trash icon** on any image to remove it

---

## ✅ Publishing Your Changes

### **After Adding Images**:
1. Scroll to bottom of page
2. Click: **"Publish"** button (green button)
3. Wait: 10 seconds
4. Go to frontend: http://localhost:3002/explore
5. Click: Your heritage site card
6. Hard refresh: **Ctrl + Shift + R**
7. Images appear! ✅

## 💡 Tips for Great Heritage Site Photos

### **Main Image Tips**:
- ✅ Show the most iconic view
- ✅ Good lighting (morning or late afternoon)
- ✅ Clear, sharp focus
- ✅ No tourists blocking the view (or minimal)
- ✅ Landscape orientation

### **Gallery Image Tips**:
- ✅ Show variety (different angles, distances, details)
- ✅ Include context (paths, signs, facilities)
- ✅ Show seasonal differences if possible
- ✅ Include people for scale (but not too many)
- ✅ Capture unique features

### **Photo Taking Advice**:
- 📸 Visit during golden hour (early morning/late afternoon)
- 📸 Use landscape mode (horizontal)
- 📸 Keep camera steady
- 📸 Take multiple shots of same view
- 📸 Capture details (water texture, plants, rocks)

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### **"Image won't upload"**
**Solutions**:
- Check file size (under 2MB)
- Check format (JPG or PNG only)
- Compress at Squoosh.app first
- Try different browser
- Check internet connection

### **"Image looks blurry"**
**Solutions**:
- Upload higher resolution image
- Don't use screenshots (use original photos)
- Check image quality before uploading
- Minimum 1200px width recommended

### **"Gallery images don't show on frontend"**
**Solutions**:
- Make sure you clicked "Publish" (not just save)
- Wait 10 seconds for cache
- Hard refresh: Ctrl + Shift + R
- Check browser console for errors (F12)
- Verify images uploaded successfully in CMS

### **"Main image missing after upload"**
**Solutions**:
- Click "Publish" button
- Refresh Sanity Studio page
- Try uploading again
- Check file wasn't corrupted
